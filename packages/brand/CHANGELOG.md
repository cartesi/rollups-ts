# @cartesi/brand

## 0.1.0-alpha.0

### Minor Changes

- aab9574: Initial release: the Cartesi Brand Kit as code, for any frontend.
  
  The four brand colours, the two typefaces and the measurements of the brand's UI language, as CSS custom properties (`@cartesi/brand/styles.css`) and as typed constants (`cartesiColors`, `cartesiFonts` and `cartesiShape`, plus the Kit's colour pairings and their contrast ratios). The typefaces, Plus Jakarta Sans and Inter, are self-hosted through Fontsource. The official logos are published as optimised SVG files under `@cartesi/brand/assets/`, and as `data:` URIs from `@cartesi/brand/logos`.
  
  It holds only what the Brand Kit specifies, with no greys, components or framework, and has no peer dependencies.
